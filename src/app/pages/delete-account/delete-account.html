Delete your Szoop account

This page is the official account-deletion request for Szoop - A lifestyle brand on Google Play and iOS devices. Use it to ask Szoop to delete the customer account tied to your mobile number.

How Szoop deletes your account

If you can still open the Szoop app, go to Profile → Delete account. That marks the account deleted and sends a request to Szoop.

If you cannot open the app, submit the form below or write to {{ site.supportEmail }} from the number or email on the account. The account is marked deleted.

What happens next

Your account is marked deleted. Old details stay in Szoop until support removes them. If you sign in again with the same number, you start as a new Szoop customer.
