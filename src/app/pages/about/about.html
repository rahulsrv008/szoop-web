Oseedy is a house-help service for people who want the home to feel looked after without chasing a cleaner, a laundry pile, or a dirty sink.

You book in the app. An expert who has been identity-checked and trained on hygiene arrives for the duration you chose. You see the price first. You rate the visit after.

The customer app, the expert app, and this website share one backend. This page is the public front: what the service is, where it is opening, and how a booking works.
